# Code of conduct

CzGram is a small project for people learning Czech and people who build tools for them. Everyone is welcome here,
whatever their level of Czech, Python or Linux.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people writing in their second or third language.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. Explain what's wrong and, where you can, how to fix it.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming a mistake was deliberate.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues first, and keep reports focused.
- Respect privacy. Don't post anyone's personal data, API keys, screenshots of private pages or school accounts.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, about anyone's language, nationality, gender, sexuality, religion, disability
  or anything else.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and commits, and anywhere you represent the
project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@unforged1](https://github.com/unforged1), on GitHub. Reports are handled privately, and the
person reporting won't be identified without their consent.

## What happens next

Depending on how serious it is, the maintainer may ask for a change in behaviour, hide or remove content, or block
someone from the project, temporarily or for good. Decisions are explained to the people involved where that's safe.
